# Meeting summaries

**Status: proposed guidelines, pending community discussion. Please don't submit meeting summaries yet.**

We haven't agreed as a community whether discussion from Kaltura-EDU meetings (such as KCEE) belongs in a public repository, or in what form. People in those meetings should be able to raise concerns and criticisms, including about Kaltura, without worrying that they'll be quoted or identified. Summaries produced by Zoom or other tools often carry names, links, recording IDs, and institution details that make this hard to guarantee.

This page describes how we propose to handle meeting material once the community is comfortable with it.

## Proposed approach

1. **Don't publish raw tool output.** Zoom and other summaries are never committed as they come, and the original email, PDF, or transcript never enters the repository.
2. **Prefer distilled claims over summaries.** Rather than a record of who discussed what, write individual claims as `Unverified` notes in `notes/`, with no attribution and a loose source such as "community discussion, 2026" instead of an exact date.
3. **If a summary is ever published, redact it first** using the [redaction checklist](../CONTRIBUTING.md#redaction-checklist), and have the meeting organizers or attendees review it before it merges.
4. **Keep candid views out.** Frank comments about the vendor or colleagues stay out unless the person who made them is happy to see them shared.
5. **Meeting content stays `Unverified`.** It consists of questions, guesses, and things individual participants saw in their own environments.

## Until this is agreed

- Don't add files to this folder.
- Don't cite a meeting as the `source` of a note.
- If you learned something in a meeting that you've also seen or tested yourself, write it up as a normal note with your own testing as the source.

Comments and objections are welcome in [Discussions](../../discussions).
